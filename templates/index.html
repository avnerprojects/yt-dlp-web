Audio formats explained

kbps (bitrate)
How much data is used per second of audio. Higher = better quality and bigger files. ~128 kbps is fine for speech & podcasts, 192 kbps is a solid all-rounder, and 256 kbps+ is best for music you care about.
m4a (AAC)
The industry-standard format. Plays everywhere — iPhone, Mac, car stereos, almost every app — with great quality for the file size. Pick this when you want maximum compatibility.
webm (Opus)
Google's open format. Usually the best quality per kilobyte, so the smallest file at a given quality. Great for modern players (VLC, most desktop/mobile apps), but less universally supported than m4a on older devices.

These describe the original audio track. This app re-encodes your pick to MP3 @ 192 kbps for maximum compatibility so choose the highest kbps available for the best result.
